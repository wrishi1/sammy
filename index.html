Our Heritage & Values

MADE WITH INTENTION.

Founded on the principle that joinery should never compete with architecture, Sammy’s creates bespoke cabinet environments that harmonize geometry, texture, and everyday utility.

Historical Milestone: Cauval Sammy Group Ltd. Partnership
Studio Heritage & Philosophy
“Good cabinetry should feel like it belongs there — designed with the architecture, not simply placed inside it.”

Our master craftspeople combine legacy European joinery techniques with advanced CNC woodworking infrastructure. From global brand partnerships such as Cauval Sammy Group to private villa commissions, every piece is engineered with sub-millimeter precision.
